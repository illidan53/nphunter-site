隐私说明

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 21 日

登录信息

你可以不登录，以游客身份使用本站。选择 Google 或 Microsoft 登录后，本站接收账号的提供商标识、用户标识、姓名和邮箱，用于建立登录会话、显示账号及判断普通用户或管理员权限。Microsoft 登录面向个人账号，使用基本个人资料权限。本站不请求读取邮件正文、联系人或日历，也不接收你的账号密码。

访问记录

本站记录页面访问时间、路径、IP 地址、国家估计、浏览器信息、语言及浏览器签名；登录后的记录还包含邮箱及用户角色。签名由浏览器的基本属性在本地哈希生成，用于近似去重，不用于登录验证。浏览器配置相同可能被合并，设置变化可能产生不同签名。统计用于了解网站使用趋势和排查问题，详细记录仅管理员可访问。

存储与期限

登录及统计数据保存在本站的 AWS 后端（美国东部区域）。访问记录默认保留 90 天，过期即从查询结果中排除，底层存储随后自动清理；备份可能在其备份窗口内继续保留。登录会话持续最多 24 小时，退出时撤销。管理员身份绑定持续保留，以防止同名邮箱被其他账号冒用。

本站使用必要的安全 Cookie 保存会话（最长 24 小时）及临时登录验证（最长 10 分钟），并在浏览器本地保存语言偏好。浏览器签名不使用画布或字体枚举，不创建跨站跟踪 Cookie。

服务提供方与选择

Google 和 Microsoft 处理各自的登录流程，AWS 提供本站的数据存储和服务运行。本站不将登录信息或访问记录出售给广告商。你可以继续使用游客模式、退出登录或清除本站浏览器数据；清除浏览器数据不会删除已保存的服务端访问记录。本站链接到的其他游戏或工具有各自的数据处理方式。

联系

如需询问个人信息的使用，或请求访问、删除与你相关的记录，请联系 shenglei.zhao.us@gmail.com。处理请求时可能需要确认账号或相关访问记录，避免向其他人披露数据。

Privacy notice

Updated September 21, 2026

Sign-in information

You can use this site as a guest. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, this site receives your identity provider, account identifier, name and email to create a session, display your account and assign member or administrator access. Microsoft sign-in supports personal accounts and requests basic profile access. We do not request email contents, contacts or calendars, and we do not receive your account password.

Visit records

We record page time and path, IP address, estimated country, browser information, language and a browser signature. Signed-in visits also record email and role. The signature hashes basic browser properties locally for approximate deduplication, never for authentication. Similar configurations can collide and changed settings can produce a different signature. Statistics help us understand site usage and troubleshoot issues. Detailed records are available only to administrators.

Storage and retention

Authentication and analytics data are stored in our AWS backend in the US East region. Visit records are retained for 90 days by default, excluded from queries on expiry and subsequently removed from underlying storage. Backups may retain data within their backup window. Sessions last up to 24 hours and are revoked on sign-out. Administrator identity bindings persist to prevent another account from claiming the same email.

Necessary secure cookies hold sessions for up to 24 hours and temporary login validation for up to 10 minutes. Your language preference is saved in browser storage. Signatures do not use canvas or font enumeration, and do not create a cross-site tracking cookie.

Providers and choices

Google and Microsoft process their respective sign-in flows; AWS hosts this site's services and data. We do not sell sign-in information or visit records to advertisers. You may remain a guest, sign out or clear this site's browser data. Clearing browser data does not delete existing server-side visit records. Linked games and tools have their own data practices.

Contact

For questions or requests to access or delete records relating to you, contact shenglei.zhao.us@gmail.com. We may need to verify your account or the relevant records to avoid disclosing data to someone else.
